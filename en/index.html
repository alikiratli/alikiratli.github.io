About me

From systems engineer to software developer and author

I was born in Bursa, Turkey. After four years at a military high school, I spent four years studying systems engineering (B. Sc.) at the Turkish Military Academy in Ankara – eight years as a boarding student, with summers spent in training camps. I then served for 15 years as a systems engineer, auditor and officer, responsible for planning, information systems, information security and training. Altogether, I spent 23 years in the army.

In 2020 I moved to Germany with my family. I learned German, retrained as an IT specialist for application development (Fachinformatiker, IT Akademie Dr. Heuer, IHK qualification 2026) and now develop my own applications – often with modern AI tools such as Claude Code.

What drives me: learning should be easier. That’s why I build apps that help people prepare for exams, and write workbooks that help children grow more confident step by step. I also write novels – from a crime series set in my hometown of Ennepetal to science fiction.

I also write on Medium – about digital forensics for financial crime in cloud and accounting systems, and about my career change from army officer to software developer.

I’m married and the father of two daughters. In my free time I play table tennis in the regional league for TV Rüggeberg and am a certified WTTV umpire. I also love camping, skiing, travelling and reading.
